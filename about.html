The Beginning

How Balanju started

I'm Balaji — a B.Tech engineering student with a love for building things with code. Balanju is my startup, started in 2026 with a simple belief:

"If I can build it, I can solve problems for real people. And if I can solve problems, I can build a business."

I started by building personal projects, learning full-stack development, and exploring AI. Over time, I realized I could turn these skills into something real — a company that creates its own products and helps other businesses with theirs.

Balanju is brand new. We're just getting started — but we're working hard, learning fast, and shipping real things.

Our Mission

"Build software that matters.
Serve clients who believe in quality."

At Balanju, every line of code we write has a purpose. Whether it's a client's website or our own product — we build things that are useful, well-designed, and built to last.
